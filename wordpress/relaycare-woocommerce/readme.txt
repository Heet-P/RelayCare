=== RelayCare for WooCommerce ===
Requires Plugins: woocommerce
Stable tag: 0.1.0
License: GPL-2.0-or-later

Adds a "Get Support" action to WooCommerce customer orders. Clicking it opens RelayCare, where the
customer verifies their email and reports a product fault against that purchase.

== Setup ==
1. Connect the store in RelayCare (Settings > Integrations).
2. Copy the RelayCare URL, Integration ID and Link secret into WooCommerce > RelayCare.
